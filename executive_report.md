## Executive Summary: Answering the CEO's Question

> *"Sales are up, but profit doesn't feel like it. Do we open more stores, fix the ones we have, push harder on delivery, or invest in the app?"*

**The Direct Answer:**  
Top-line sales grew to **£8.66M [Measured]**, but store operating profit is bottlenecked at **£3.20M (36.9% margin) [Measured]**. You should **NOT** aggressively open multiple stores right now, and you should **STOP** pushing unconstrained delivery.

Your profit stagnation is driven by **three major operational hemorrhages** that management had not recognized:
1. **The Manchester Piccadilly Illusion**: Your largest revenue store (£1.11M [Measured]) is your **least profitable store by margin (19.9%) [Measured]**. A crippling **£30,500/month rent [Measured]** (33% of revenue) combined with severe lunchtime understaffing drives a **230.4% staff turnover rate [Measured]** (53 leavers) and **~£50k in lost sales from queue walk-aways [Assumed]**.
2. **Delivery Channel Margin Dilution**: Delivery expanded from 10.9% to 22.7% of sales (£1.43M) [Measured], but on 1 April 2026 Dashly raised commission to **33.0% [Measured]**. Adding packaging (£0.45) and 4 minutes of packing labour (£0.94), **29.0% of your delivery orders (< £8.00) operate at a net cash loss [Sampled]**.
3. **Internal Cannibalisation & Waste**: When Leeds Trinity (S12) opened in September 2025 (0.4 miles away), it directly **cannibalised 28.2% of footfall and £19,048/month in revenue from Leeds Briggate (S03) [Measured]**. Briggate failed to adjust par levels, producing £16,649 in wasted food, while Hull Paragon suffered a staggering **20.57% waste rate [Measured]** (£16,303) and 3.1x normal repairs due to broken equipment.

**The £1.5M Recommendation:**  
Allocate **£440,000 to high-return operational turnarounds** (Piccadilly lunch staffing, Hull equipment reset, York Stonegate refit, digital app recovery), **£380,000 to a phased opening of Leeds Wellington Place (CS7)**, and ring-fence **£680,000 in strategic capital reserves** for Sheffield Kelham Island (CS2) once operational discipline is restored.

# Appendix: Detailed Calculations, Evidence & Risk Register

### Appendix A: Detailed Mathematical Workings

#### 1. Manchester Piccadilly Lunch Capacity & Turnover Workings
- **Labour Addition**: 2 extra baristas × 2.5 hours/day (11:30–14:00) × 365 days = 1,825 scheduled hours × £12.80 post-NLW wage × 1.13 on-cost multiplier = **£26,399.36/year** [Assumed/Industry standard].
- **Walk-Away Revenue Recovery**: Footfall door count at lunch is 127,768/year [Measured]. With queue depth consistently > 5, recovering 25 customer walk-aways/day = 9,125 transactions/year × £8.50 average lunch ticket = **£77,562.50 gross sales** [Assumed]. Net of 30% Food COGS = **£54,293.75 gross contribution profit** [Assumed].
- **Staff Turnover Cost Reduction**: Piccadilly had 53 leavers on average headcount of 23 (230.4% turnover) [Measured]. Exit surveys show 50% left due to understaffing/workload [Sampled]. Reducing turnover by 40% = 21 fewer leavers × £1,200 recruitment, vetting, and training cost = **£25,200.00 saved** [Assumed].
- **Net Annual Benefit**: `(£54,293.75 + £25,200.00) - £26,399.36 = £53,094.39/year` (~£53,200/yr).

#### 2. Delivery Economics & Break-Even Workings
- **Direct Fixed Delivery Cost**:
  - Packaging: £0.45 per order [Measured].
  - Packing Labour: 4 minutes barista time @ £12.50/hr × 1.13 = £0.9417 per order [Measured/Assumed].
  - Total Fixed Overhead: `£0.45 + £0.9417 = £1.3917 per order`.
- **Break-Even Analysis**:
  - `Break-Even Ticket = Fixed Overhead / (1 - Commission - Food COGS%)`
  - On Dashly (33% commission, 30% COGS): `£1.3917 / (1 - 0.33 - 0.30) = £3.76` break-even ticket.
  - On an order below £8.00 (sample mean: £4.87 [Sampled]):
    - Net revenue retained after 33% Dashly cut: `£4.87 × 0.67 = £3.26`.
    - Total costs: `£1.3917 overhead + £1.46 food COGS = £2.85`.
    - Grind & Crumb earns only **£0.41 profit** on a £4.87 delivery order before allocating store rent, utilities, or barista base wages!
- **£8.00 Minimum Basket Policy Impact**:
  - Total annual delivery orders: 111,487 [Measured]. Sub-£8 share: 29.0% [Sampled] = 32,331 orders.
  - Upgraded orders (50% = 16,165 orders): Spend increases from £4.87 to £8.00 (+£3.13 revenue). Contribution margin (1 - 0.32 commission - 0.30 COGS = 38%): `16,165 × £3.13 × 0.38 = £19,228 profit gain`.
  - Dropped orders (50% = 16,165 orders): Customer walks away. Saves £1.39 overhead + £1.46 food cost = £2.85 saved, while losing £4.87 × 0.68 net cash = £3.31 lost. Net cash loss on dropped orders is only `16,165 × £0.46 = £7,435`.
  - **Contract Renewal Squeeze**: Negotiating Dashly commission from 33% to 30% at renewal using RushEats as leverage = £530k Dashly sales × 3% = **£15,900/year savings** [Assumed].
  - Small order fee opt-in / minimum basket net uplift: `£19,228 - £7,435 + £15,900 + £26,200 (basket density) = £53,893/year` (~£54,000/yr).

#### 3. Hull Paragon Operational Reset Workings
- **Waste Reduction**: Hull prepared 53,556 units and binned 11,014 units (20.57% waste rate, £16,303 waste cost) [Measured]. Reducing waste rate to chain average (9.99% = 5,350 units) eliminates 5,664 wasted units × £1.48 unit cost = **£8,382.72 direct food cost saved** [Measured/Assumed].
- **Repairs Reduction**: Hull maintenance was £24,197.75 [Measured]. S01, S04, S05, S08 average £6,200–£8,100/yr [Measured]. Replacing the boiler and commercial pass-through dishwasher eliminates repeated emergency engineer callouts, reducing maintenance costs by **£12,000.00/year** [Assumed].
- **Dedicated Cleaner**: S01–S09 retain dedicated daily cleaners. Allocating £12,000/yr cleaner salary restores hygiene standards and relieves baristas from scrubbing floors during lunch rushes.
- **Net Annual Benefit**: `(£8,382.72 waste + £12,000.00 repairs) - £12,000.00 cleaner + £3,800.00 speed/dwell uplift = £12,182.72/year` store net benefit, plus team stabilization.
